The Client library owns identity and Router client capabilities. Registry remains private and Router becomes private; Client and OpenClaw are the published release set.

Imports map from `@moltzap/identity` to `@moltzap/client` for host values, from `@moltzap/identity/registry` and `@moltzap/router` to `@moltzap/client/service`, and from `@moltzap/identity/internal` to condition-only `@moltzap/client/internal`. Services import named Registry wire grammar there (as `RegistryApi`) alongside verification and Router contracts.
